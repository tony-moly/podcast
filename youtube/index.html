Palata 404 Publisher

Palata 404 Publisher is a private tool used by the author of the "Palata 404" podcast and the Source Kitchen channel to publish the author's own short explainer videos.

The author makes vertical videos about how technology works. The tool uploads each finished video, with its title and description, to the author's own YouTube channel as a Short. It uses the YouTube API Services only to upload videos and to read the name of the connected channel. It does not post on behalf of anyone else, does not read other users' content and does not collect data about viewers.

How it works

The channel owner signs in with Google once and grants permission to upload videos. After that the tool uploads videos the owner has already approved. The owner can revoke access at any time on the Google security settings page.
