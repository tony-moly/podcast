Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Palata 404 Publisher ("the app") is a personal publishing tool. Its only user is the owner of the connected YouTube channel, who also operates the app. The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app receives

When the owner signs in with Google, the app receives an access token, a refresh token and the ID and title of the owner's YouTube channel. The app does not receive passwords, contacts, comments, viewing history or information about other YouTube users.

How the data is used

The tokens are used only to upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's own channel. The channel title is shown to the owner to confirm which channel is connected.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored on the owner's own computer and are not sent to any third party. The app does not sell, rent or share data, does not use it for advertising and does not track viewers. The app does not place cookies or similar technologies on users' devices.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the channel stays connected. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page. After that the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the app within 7 days. The owner can also ask for deletion through the contact below.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from them.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
