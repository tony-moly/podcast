Palata 404 Publisher

Palata 404 Publisher is a private tool used by the author of the "Palata 404" podcast and the Source Kitchen channel to publish his own short explainer videos.

The author makes vertical videos about how technology works. The tool uploads each finished video, with its caption, to the author's own TikTok account at a scheduled time. It does not post on behalf of anyone else, does not read other users' content and does not collect data about viewers.

How it works

The account owner signs in with TikTok once and grants permission to publish videos. After that the tool uploads videos the owner has already approved, on the owner's schedule. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings under Security and permissions, Apps and services.
