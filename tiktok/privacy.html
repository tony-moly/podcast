Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Palata 404 Publisher ("the app") is a personal publishing tool. Its only user is the owner of the connected TikTok account, who also operates the app.

What data the app receives

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID and basic profile information (display name and avatar). The app does not receive passwords, contacts, messages or information about other TikTok users.

How the data is used

The tokens are used only to upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's own account and to check the status of those uploads. Profile information is shown to the owner to confirm which account is connected.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored on the owner's own computer and are not sent to any third party. The app does not sell, rent or share data, does not use it for advertising and does not track viewers.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account stays connected. The owner can disconnect the app in TikTok settings under Security and permissions, Apps and services. After that the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from them.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
